About this course

Welcome to CS 4520/5520! In this course, you're going to learn how to build iOS apps from the ground up using the modern Swift stack. We'll be diving into tools like UIKit, Material 3, MVC Architecture, Delegates, and Storyboards. You'll also get hands-on experience with Retrofit, Firebase (Auth, Firestore, and Storage), Room, DataStore, Maps, CameraX, and basic testing.

The course is completely online and asynchronous, meaning you can learn at your own pace! Each module covers a new topic and includes lecture videos, reading materials, a guided lab, a code walkthrough, and a graded assignment. While there are no mandatory live sessions, our instructional team—TAs and your instructor—holds weekly drop-in Zoom office hours whenever you need help. You can also always jump into Piazza, our main discussion hub. Everything builds up to a semester-long team project where you'll submit a demo video showcasing what you've built.

Want to take things a step further? There's an optional Kotlin Multiplatform module if you're interested in extending your shared code to run on iOS.
